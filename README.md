# Barrel Roll Bullet

**Aim, shoot, spin and soar!** Navigate your way through levels using a large variety of abilities to help you along the way.

This game is not easy, excelling requires a fast reaction time!

### Installation

1. Click the download link above.
2. Open the downloaded `.zip` file.
3. Select **Extract All**.
4. Open the extracted **Barrel Roll Bullet** folder.
5. Open `!BARREL ROLL BULLET GAME!.exe`.
6. Play!

## How to Play

Barrel Roll Bullet is a recoil-based platformer where your movement is controlled by shooting your gun.

Use your shots to move through the levels, avoid obstacles, use different abilities, and reach the goal.

### Controls

* **Mouse** - Aim
* **Left Click/Enter** - Shoot
* **A** - Re-align player
  * **R** - Hold to restart level


## Features

1. **Polygon-based SAT collision system**
2. **Multiple ability pickups with varying effects**
3. **Recoil-based movement system**
4. **50 levels with varying difficulty**
5. **Multiple game states and interactive menus**
6. **Custom level mechanics including portals, gates and breakable tiles**
